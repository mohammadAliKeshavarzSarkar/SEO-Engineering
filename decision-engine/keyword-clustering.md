# Keyword clustering decision protocol
Use after R03 and before R06. This is a review procedure, not a publication authorization.

| Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|
| Only orthographic or semantic similarity | Candidate relationship; inspect intent and search evidence |
| Comparable complete snapshots, sufficient overlap, same task and constraints | Propose same group subject to review of offering and page fit |
| Strong overlap but materially different tasks, constraints or language scopes | Keep separate or investigate; no automatic merge |
| Missing/inconsistent snapshots or uncertain intent | Keep provisional/unknown, request appropriate evidence |
| Bridge query A–B–C without A–C compatibility | Review each pair; do not merge by transitivity |
| Existing pages share query impressions but serve distinct useful tasks | Preserve; investigate whether any measurable harm exists |
| Verified duplicate content or genuine redundant task | Propose differentiation/consolidation; do not deploy without approval |

Document pair evidence and actual scope, threshold and limitations, negative examples, cluster purpose, current pages, rationale and reviewer. A cluster does not necessarily equal a page. For mixed or competing membership, record alternatives and unresolved needs. Follow [R04](../docs/02-research/r04-keyword-normalization-and-clustering.md) for method, [review template](../templates/keyword-cluster-review.csv) for output, and [migration guide](../docs/03-technical/urls-and-migrations.md) before any approved URL changes.
