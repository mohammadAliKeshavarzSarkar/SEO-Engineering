# Technical audit decision protocol

Use [T10](../docs/03-technical/t10-audit-automation.md). Confirm scope/authority, project intent, actual capability, reviewed input and coverage first. Select only meaningful modules; blocked/not applicable/failed never pass. Execute trusted local functions only; retain native reports and exact signal refs. Investigate suspected security/access exposure promptly independent of grouping. Separate deferred data from affirmative review candidates and verified issues. Group only after comparing real route identity, context, rule purpose and likely shared cause; do not merge matching labels or replace conflicting data. Keep ungrouped and unresolved evidence. Use existing R08 with reviewed severity, confidence, fit, risk, effort and dependencies; no generated business score. Propose smallest reversible action or no change, with owner, evidence, acceptance and rollback. No approval inferred from CLI success. Separate real technical/processed/business verification.
