# Competitor intelligence to opportunity decision

Use after R01–R04 and before R06/R07. Produces a proposal, not authorization to publish or modify a site.

| Evidence / condition | Decision |
|---|---|
| Repeated in search sample but different business | Retain as search/content benchmark; no commercial share conclusion |
| Same buyers and offering but absent from sample | Retain business comparator; report absence within sample only |
| Scope mismatch, partial data, blocked page | Mark unknown/not comparable; seek appropriate evidence |
| Competitor serves task unrelated to offering | Reject; investigate suitable tasks |
| Target has useful adequate page | Preserve; investigate visibility/technical gaps if relevant |
| Evidenced user task or content shortfall | Improve existing page with original value and measurable test |
| Distinct unsatisfied task aligned with offering | Propose new useful page/tool; route to R06/R07 |
| Feature or claim cannot be supported | Reject or defer pending product/expert evidence |
| Rival's risky tactic or links seem correlated with visibility | Investigate lawful independent value; no causal inference or copying |

Review actual task, business fit, own page inventory, comparable evidence, independent contribution, alternatives including no-change, owner/feasibility, privacy, risk, acceptance and outcome. Use [opportunity template](../templates/competitor-opportunities.csv), [decision record](../templates/decision-record.md) and [R05 methodology](../docs/02-research/r05-competitor-intelligence.md). Unknown is not evidence of inferiority. Final ownership and priority require later research and approval.
