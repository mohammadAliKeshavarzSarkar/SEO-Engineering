Proposed phases: scope and baseline; keyword/competitor research with Persian variants; eligibility and rendering; URL/information architecture; content and trust; search appearance; performance; privacy-aware measurement and lead QA; authority and AI visibility; ongoing experiments. Critical production problems and measurement setup can be addressed earlier.
