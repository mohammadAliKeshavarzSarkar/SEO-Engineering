# Worked R03 example — synthetic only

All scenarios, counts and URLs below are invented for learning. They are not search observations, actual Google rankings, search volume or recommendations for a real website.

## Case A: an existing adequate page

Fictional office furniture shop at example.org. Task: evaluate candidate phrase "buy ergonomic office chairs". Two comparable imagined snapshots each contain ten comparable organic result entries; assume all relevant landing pages inspected. Each contains seven categories, two product pages, one guide. Shopping units and ads are recorded separately and not included in the count. All locations/devices are hypothetical matched settings. Existing /chairs/ergonomic/ has useful stock, filters, dimensions, delivery and clear navigation.

- Primary task hypothesis: transact/shop, category browsing plausible. Secondary: compare. Confidence: high conditional on these hypothetical observations; no claim about real market.
- Seven of ten sampled pages are categories in each capture. This does not mean 70% of people buy or that Google requires a category.
- Existing page ownership: candidate /chairs/ergonomic/, confirm query/page performance if available.
- Decision: retain adequate page and inspect meaningful gaps. Do not create synonym landing pages merely for wording. Verification: compare existing page usefulness, index eligibility and later aligned search and business outcomes.
- Next: R04 investigate related variants before final R06 mapping.

## Case B: incomplete mixed-intent data

Fictional solar company; broad phrase has generic web-search feed with three guides and three vendors, but no verified engine rank, market, device or result features. The site already has a guide and a category. Evidence supports preliminary mixed possibilities only; exact local position and feature presence are unknown. Low intent confidence and unknown owner confidence. Keep the distinct current roles provisionally; collect permissioned localized snapshots and relevant first-party data before deciding. Do not make six-result feed a rank tracker, claim user-intent shares or merge the pages.

## Case C: high demand but wrong business

Fictional remote invoicing product. Imagined local search for "accountant near me" shows map/local results and actual local offices; the product has no accounting-office service. Local intent plausible, business fit absent. Out-of-scope decision even if a demand vendor reports high volume. Do not manufacture city offices, doorway pages or service claims. Research remote invoicing tasks aligned with real offering instead.
