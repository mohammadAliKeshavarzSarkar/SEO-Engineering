# Authority, promotion and links

Earn citations through original resources, reliable expertise, product documentation, industry collaboration, tools and genuine case studies. Build outreach around value for the recipient's audience, not exact-match anchor demands. Case studies require permission, context, methodology, limitations and real results. Do not buy links to manipulate rankings, participate in private networks, fabricate mentions or publish unrelated third-party content primarily to exploit domain authority.

Link qualification: rel=sponsored for paid placements (nofollow is also accepted by Google for paid links); rel=ugc for applicable user-generated links; nofollow where association is not intended. Use reasonable editorial links without unnecessary restrictions. Review backlinks for genuine problems; do not automatically disavow ordinary low-quality links. Disavow is exceptional and requires current platform guidance and informed review.

Track earned referral traffic, relevant mentions, partnerships, useful content distribution and conversion contribution. Domain authority scores are third-party estimates, not search engine scores. Review risks, editorial independence, consent and copyright before publishing.

Sources: [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), [qualify outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links).
