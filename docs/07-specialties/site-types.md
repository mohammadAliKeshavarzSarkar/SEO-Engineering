# Specialty playbooks by website type

Apply only the relevant modules; each requires project-specific research.

## Local business

Validate real service locations/areas, business eligibility and accurate names, categories, hours/contact details. Maintain Google Business Profile where supported and legitimate. Location pages need specific service/proof and user value, not city-name substitution. Earn real reviews without manipulation; respond professionally; measure calls, directions and qualified enquiries. Check platform country availability and address-display rules. Source: [Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177).

## E-commerce

Inventory category, product, variant, filter, pagination, retired and out-of-stock URLs. Decide which facets satisfy real intent; constrain unbounded combinations without blocking useful discovery. Preserve meaningful variant URL and canonical policy; distinguish temporary stock status from permanent retirement. Accurate visible product offer, price, currency and availability must agree with eligible structured data and Merchant Center feed if used. Avoid fake reviews or thin copied manufacturer pages. Track purchase/refund and inventory state without PII. Source: [ecommerce SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce).

## SaaS and B2B

Map solutions, audience/use case, integrations, comparisons, docs and onboarding topics to distinct intent; avoid superficial competitor pages and unsupported claims. Keep authenticated dashboards private. Measure trials, accepted demo leads and qualified pipeline; protect sensitive data. Provide real product proof and transparent compatibility/pricing where appropriate.

## Publishers and marketplaces

Publishers: visible bylines, corrections, publication dates, meaningful archives and content taxonomy; review duplicate syndication and eligible news/discover features. Marketplaces: ensure listing quality and useful category pages, moderate UGC, handle expired listings and control faceted URL growth. Avoid indexing empty or low-value pages automatically.

## Programmatic SEO

Only generate pages when each has genuine distinct data/value and a useful journey. Establish a quality threshold, canonical URL registry, publication limits, coverage of empty states, manual sample review, removal rules and monitoring. Reject doorway or thin near-duplicate pages regardless of scale. A template alone is not an SEO strategy. Source: [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
